ChitPick — The Digital Chit Picker for Friends

ChitPick is a free online random name picker and digital chit picking game. Add your friends names, set a custom task like who pays the bill or who washes dishes, and drop beautiful animated paper chits on a virtual table. Tap any chit to reveal the chosen person — fair, fun, and instant.

Perfect for friend groups, roommates, families, and offices. No sign-up needed. Works on mobile and desktop. Try the best who-pays-the-bill app online — completely free.
